Personality & Background
Personality
A Forest Gnome of extraordinary self-regard. Balthazar performs under the name “El Ballo,” which he considers far more befitting of his talent than anything nature gave him. He speaks of himself in superlatives, dresses in the finest clothes available, and regards every audience as lucky to have him.
Flaw
Arrogant and snobbish. Used to luxury. If lodgings are below aristocratic standard, he makes his displeasure loudly known.
Lifestyle
Aristocratic — 10 gp/day minimum.
Features & Traits
Spellcasting
Cast bard spells using Charisma. Use a musical instrument as a spellcasting focus. Bard spells are always known — no daily preparation required.
Jack of All Trades
Add half proficiency (+2) to any ability check that doesn’t already include your proficiency bonus.
Song of Rest
Allies who spend HD during a short rest and can hear your performance heal an extra 1d8 HP.
Expertise
Double proficiency bonus in 4 chosen skills (2 at level 3, 2 at level 10).
Magical Secrets
Learned Spirit Guardians and Counterspell from outside the bard spell list (neither is on the bard list natively). Greater Restoration is a native bard spell and needs no MS slot. +2 more picks at levels 14 and 18.
Natural Illusionist (Gnome)
Know Minor Illusion as a cantrip, using Intelligence as the spellcasting ability.
Speak with Small Beasts (Gnome)
Communicate simple ideas with Small or smaller beasts through sounds and gestures.

By Popular Demand Background Feature Entertainer
I can always find a place to perform — inn, tavern, circus, noble court — where I receive free lodging and food of a modest or comfortable standard, as long as I perform each night.

My performances make me something of a local figure. When strangers recognize me in a town where I have performed, they typically take a liking to me.
