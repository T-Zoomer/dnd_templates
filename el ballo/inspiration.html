Feature Descriptions
Bardic Inspiration Short Rest Bonus Action
As a bonus action, choose one creature other than yourself within 60 ft that can hear you. That creature gains one Bardic Inspiration die: d10.
  • Within the next 10 minutes, the creature can roll the die once and add it to one ability check, attack roll, or saving throw. They can decide after seeing the d20 result but before the DM says if it succeeds.
  • Only one creature can hold a Bardic Inspiration die at a time — you can’t have a stockpile out there.
  • You have 4 uses per short rest (Charisma modifier +4). Font of Inspiration means all 4 reset on a short rest, not just a long rest.
Mantle of Inspiration Uses Inspiration Die Bonus Action
As a bonus action, expend one Bardic Inspiration die. Choose up to 4 creatures (your Charisma modifier) within 60 ft that can see and hear you.
  • Each chosen creature gains 11 temporary HP (fixed value at level 10 — not a die roll: 5 at L3, 8 at L5, 11 at L10, 14 at L15).
  • Each creature can then immediately use its reaction to move up to its speed, without provoking opportunity attacks.
This uses your one Bardic Inspiration die — using it for Mantle of Inspiration means it’s gone until your next short rest.
Enthralling Performance Short Rest
If you perform for at least 1 minute, you can attempt to charm your audience. Choose up to 4 humanoids (your CHA mod) within 60 ft that watched and listened the entire time. Each must make a Wisdom save (DC 16).
  • On a fail: the creature is charmed for 1 hour. While charmed: it is enthralled by you, hinders those who oppose you, and avoids violence against you and your allies.
  • The charmed creature doesn’t know you tried to charm it — it thinks it was simply impressed.
  • The charm ends if the target takes damage, if you attack it, or if you attack one of its allies.
Mantle of Majesty Long Rest Bonus Action
As a bonus action, you appear unearthly beautiful while you concentrate for up to 1 minute (concentration, can be ended early as a free action).
  • During this time, as a bonus action on each of your turns, you can cast Command without expending a spell slot.
  • Any creature charmed by you automatically fails its saving throw against any Command you cast.
  • This lasts until the end of your next turn unless you use another bonus action to extend it (each bonus action extends by 1 turn).
Combo potential: charm a creature with Enthralling Performance, then activate Mantle of Majesty the next round — that creature auto-fails all your Commands.
Countercharm No Resource • Action
As an action, start a performance that lasts until the end of your next turn (you can extend it each turn by using another action, or end it at any time as a free action).
  • While performing, any friendly creature within 30 ft that can hear you has advantage on saving throws against being frightened or charmed.
  • Uses no resources — can be repeated as often as needed.
Font of Inspiration Passive
You regain your Bardic Inspiration uses after a short or long rest (not just a long rest). This means the single d10 Inspiration die comes back every time you take a short rest. Don’t hoard it.
