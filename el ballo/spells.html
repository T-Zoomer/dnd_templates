How Known Spells Work (Bard)
▶
Always available — no preparation needed Unlike a druid or wizard, you don’t choose spells each morning. All 12 of your known spells plus 4 cantrips are always available to cast. You can’t swap them out except by levelling up.
▶
Magical Secrets spells Spirit Guardians and Greater Restoration are marked MS — they are not normally on the bard spell list. You learned them through Magical Secrets at level 10.
▶
Forest Gnome spells are separate Minor Illusion from your Gnome heritage uses Intelligence as the spellcasting ability (DC 11, +3 attack), not Charisma. It has no slots and can be cast freely.
How Using Spells Works
▶
Spell slots are your fuel You have 4/3/3/3/2 slots (levels 1–5). Each time you cast a non-cantrip spell, you expend one slot of that level or higher. All slots reset on a long rest.
▶
Upcasting You can cast a lower-level spell using a higher-level slot for a stronger effect. Each spell notes what changes when upcast.
▶
Concentration Spells marked Conc require concentration. You can only concentrate on one spell at a time — casting a second ends the first. Taking damage forces a Con save (DC 10 or half damage, whichever is higher) or you lose concentration.
▶
Counterspell is a reaction You can cast Counterspell as a reaction when you see a creature within 60 ft casting a spell. No slot needed for 3rd level or lower — automatic. For 4th level or higher, make a Charisma check (DC 10 + spell level) — your +4 CHA mod helps here.
▶
Spell save DC & attack bonus When a spell forces a save, the enemy rolls vs. DC 16. When a spell requires an attack roll, you roll d20 +8 vs. their AC.

Cantrips — always available, no slot needed
Mending
School Transmutation Time 1 minute Range Touch Duration Instantaneous Components V, S, M (two lodestones)
Repair a single break or tear in an object you touch, up to 1 foot in any dimension. Leaves no trace of former damage. Can physically repair a magic item or construct, but can’t restore magic to such an object.
Minor Illusion
School Illusion Time 1 action Range 30 ft Duration 1 minute Components S, M (fleece)
Create a sound or an image of an object within range. Sound: any sound of your choice, from a whisper to a scream, originating from a point you choose. Image: a visual image no larger than a 5-ft cube. It can’t create sound, light, smell, or other sensory effects. A creature that uses its action to examine the image can detect the illusion with a successful Investigation check vs. your spell DC.
Prestidigitation
School Transmutation Time 1 action Range 10 ft Duration Up to 1 hour Components V, S
Minor magical tricks. Choose one: create a small sensory effect (shower of sparks, puff of wind, faint music); instantly light or snuff a candle, torch, or small campfire; instantly clean or soil an object no larger than 1 cubic foot; chill, warm, or flavour up to 1 cubic foot of nonliving material for 1 hour; make a colour, small mark, or symbol appear on an object for 1 hour; create a nonmagical trinket or illusory image that fits in your hand and lasts until end of next turn.
Vicious Mockery Wis save
School Enchantment Time 1 action Range 60 ft Duration Instantaneous Components V only
Unleash a string of insults laced with subtle enchantments at one creature you can see within range. The creature must succeed on a Wisdom save or take 2d4 psychic damage and have disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the end of its next turn.
1st Level — 4 slots
Command Wis save
School Enchantment Time 1 action Range 60 ft Duration 1 round Components V only
Speak a one-word command to a creature you can see within range. It must succeed on a Wisdom save or follow the command on its next turn. The spell has no effect if the target is undead, doesn’t understand your language, or if your command is directly harmful to it. Examples: approach, drop, flee, grovel, halt.
Higher levels: +1 target per slot level above 1st (targets must be within 30 ft of each other).
Faerie Fire ConcDex save
School Evocation Time 1 action Range 60 ft Duration Conc, 1 minute Components V only
Each object in a 20-ft cube within range is outlined in blue, green, or violet light. Creatures in the area that fail a Dexterity save are also outlined. Outlined creatures and objects shed dim light in a 10-ft radius. Attack rolls against outlined targets have advantage, and outlined creatures can’t benefit from being invisible.
Healing Word Bonus action
School Evocation Time 1 bonus action Range 60 ft Duration Instantaneous Components V only
A creature you can see within range regains 1d4 − 1 HP (minimum 0). No effect on undead or constructs. Bonus action means you can still cast another spell this turn, as long as it’s a cantrip.
Higher levels: +1d4 per slot level above 1st.
2nd Level — 3 slots
Suggestion ConcWis save
School Enchantment Time 1 action Range 30 ft Duration Conc, 8 hours Components V, M (snake’s tongue, honeycomb)
Suggest a course of activity (limited to a sentence or two) to a creature that can hear and understand you. It must make a Wisdom save or pursue the suggestion to the best of its ability for the duration. The suggestion must be worded so that it sounds reasonable — asking it to harm itself, or to do something obviously against its nature, ends the spell. The spell ends early if you or your companions harm the target.
3rd Level — 3 slots
Counterspell Reaction
School Abjuration Time 1 reaction* Range 60 ft Duration Instantaneous Components S only Trigger When a creature within 60 ft casts a spell
Attempt to interrupt a creature in the process of casting a spell. If the spell is 3rd level or lower, it automatically fails. If the spell is 4th level or higher, make an ability check using your spellcasting ability (Charisma), DC 10 + spell level. On success, the creature’s spell fails. Note: Creatures charmed by Mantle of Majesty automatically fail saves vs. Command, but Counterspell is separate.
Higher levels: Automatically counters spells up to the slot level used (4th, 5th, etc.).
Hypnotic Pattern ConcWis save
School Illusion Time 1 action Range 120 ft Duration Conc, 1 minute Components S, M (glowing stick or phosphorescent moss)
Create a twisting pattern of colours in a 30-ft cube within range. Each creature that can see the pattern must make a Wisdom save or be charmed for the duration. While charmed, the creature is incapacitated and has speed 0. The effect ends for an affected creature if it takes damage or if another creature uses its action to shake it out of its stupor.
Major Image Conc
School Illusion Time 1 action Range 120 ft Duration Conc, 10 minutes Components V, S, M (fleece)
Create the image of an object, creature, or other visible phenomenon up to a 20×20×20 ft cube. The image appears at a spot within range and lasts for the duration. It seems real in all senses — sight, sound, smell, temperature — but can’t cause damage or actually affect the physical world. A creature that interacts with the illusion or succeeds on an Investigation check vs. DC 11 realises it’s fake. You can move the image anywhere within range as an action.
6th level slot: The spell lasts until dispelled and doesn’t require concentration. You can then dismiss it as an action.
Spirit Guardians ConcWis saveMagical Secrets
School Conjuration Time 1 action Range Self (15-ft radius) Duration Conc, 10 minutes Components V, S, M (holy symbol)
Spectral spirits swirl around you in a 15-ft radius. Choose creatures to affect when casting. Affected creatures entering the area for the first time on a turn or starting their turn there must make a Wisdom save: fail = 3d8 radiant or necrotic damage and speed halved; success = half damage, speed not halved.
Higher levels: +1d8 damage per slot level above 3rd.
4th Level — 3 slots
Polymorph ConcWis save
School Transmutation Time 1 action Range 60 ft Duration Conc, 1 hour Components V, S, M (cocoon)
Transform a creature you can see within range into a new form. An unwilling creature must succeed on a Wisdom save to resist. The transformation can be into any beast with a CR equal to or less than the target’s CR (or challenge rating, if it doesn’t have a level). Game statistics are replaced by those of the chosen beast, but the creature retains its alignment and personality. The creature reverts if it drops to 0 HP, and any excess damage carries over to its normal form. Can be used on yourself — a powerful escape or utility option.
5th Level — 2 slots
Greater Restoration Magical Secrets
School Abjuration Time 1 action Range Touch Duration Instantaneous Components V, S, M (diamond dust worth 100 gp, consumed)
Imbue a creature you touch with positive energy to undo a debilitating effect. Choose one: end one charmed or petrified condition; end one curse (including attunement to a cursed item); end one reduction to an ability score; end one effect reducing hit point maximum; or reduce the target’s exhaustion level by one. Note: Requires 100 gp worth of diamond dust each cast.
Modify Memory ConcWis save
School Enchantment Time 1 action Range 30 ft Duration Conc, 1 minute Components V, S
Attempt to reshape another creature’s memories. The target must make a Wisdom save or be charmed for the duration. While charmed, the target is incapacitated and unaware of its surroundings (though it can hear you). During that time, you can reach into the target’s mind and alter one memory that occurred within the last 24 hours (up to 10 minutes long): suppress the memory, allow the creature to recall it with perfect clarity, change its details, or fabricate a false memory. When the spell ends, the target has no knowledge of being charmed.
6th level: memories up to 7 days. 7th: 30 days. 8th: 1 year. 9th: any time.
Synaptic Static Int save
School Enchantment Time 1 action Range 120 ft Duration 1 minute Components V, S
Choose a point you can see within range and cause psychic energy to explode there. Each creature in a 20-ft-radius sphere must make an Intelligence save. On a fail: 8d6 psychic damage, and for the next minute they must roll a d6 and subtract the result from attack rolls, ability checks, and concentration saves. On a success: half damage, no ongoing penalty. No concentration required — it just lasts 1 minute on failed targets.
Forest Gnome Racial Spell — Intelligence-based (DC 13, +5 attack)
Minor Illusion Always available • Int-based
Ability Intelligence (not Charisma) DC 13 (8 + 4 prof + 1 INT) Range 30 ft Duration 1 minute Components S, M (fleece)
Identical to the bard cantrip version above, but this copy uses Intelligence as the casting ability. You effectively have Minor Illusion twice — one CHA-based, one INT-based — both free to cast with no slot. You can only concentrate on one active illusion at a time.
