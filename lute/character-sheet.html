Personality & Background
Personality
Grew up in the middle of a big city, but developed a deep bond with animals over time — his family ran the local zoo. They did it purely for money. He was the odd one out because he actually cared. Around eighteen it became too much: he released as many animals as possible. His family threw him out. Now he’s a fledgling druid on the road, trying to do right by nature and bring humanity “into balance.”
Ideal — Harmony
Humans and nature should live in harmony. That’s all. Everyone just needs to chill.
Bond — Empathy
Animals have feelings too. Full stop.
Flaw — Naivety
Still learning that not everyone wants to join hands and sing “Imagine.”
Features & Traits
Druidic
You know Druidic, the secret language of druids. You can speak it and leave hidden messages. Others notice a message exists but can’t decipher it without the language.
Spellcasting
Cast prepared druid spells using Wisdom. Ritual casting available for spells with the ritual tag. Use druidic focus as spellcasting focus.
Combat Wild Shape
Use Wild Shape as a bonus action. While transformed, use bonus action to expend a spell slot and regain 1d8 HP per slot level. Prefers animal form in combat — that’s nature, so it’s okay.
Merchant’s Eye
Years around a money-driven family taught him to spot when people only care about profit. He’s wary of commerce and can usually tell when a deal stinks.

Stone Crest Wondrous Item Quest Object
A flat, round stone engraved with the image of a three-pointed star. On the nights of the new moon, the star seems to glimmer with emerald light.

The symbols upon it represent a primal or divine power of harmony and nature. The crest can aid its bearer in restoring the sanctity of wild places.
