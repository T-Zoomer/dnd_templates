How Preparing Spells Works
▶
Choose each morning After a long rest, you choose which spells from the full druid list to have prepared for the day. You can swap out your choices every long rest — you’re not locked in permanently.
▶
How many you can prepare Your maximum is Wisdom modifier + Druid level = 3 + 3 = 6 spells. Only prepared spells can be cast using spell slots.
▶
Cantrips are always on Guidance and Mending don’t take up prepared slots. You always have them, no preparation needed.
▶
Ritual spells are special Spells marked Ritual can be cast without preparing them, as long as they’re on the druid list. It takes 10 extra minutes and doesn’t use a spell slot. Useful for utility spells you don’t want burning a prepared slot.
How Using Spells Works
▶
Spell slots are your fuel You have 4 first-level spell slots and 2 second-level slots. Each time you cast a prepared spell, you expend one slot of the appropriate level or higher. Once all slots are gone, you can’t cast any more prepared spells until you take a long rest.
▶
Upcasting You now have 2nd-level slots. You can cast any 1st-level spell using a 2nd-level slot for a stronger effect — look for the Higher levels note on each spell.
▶
Concentration Spells marked Conc require concentration. You can only concentrate on one spell at a time — casting a second one ends the first. Taking damage forces a Constitution save (DC 10 or half damage taken, whichever is higher) or you lose concentration.
▶
Spell save DC When a spell forces an enemy to make a saving throw, they roll d20 + their relevant modifier against your DC of 13. If they roll 12 or lower they fail.
▶
Spell attack bonus Some spells (like Ice Knife) require you to make a spell attack roll: roll d20 + +5 against the target’s AC.
▶
Slots reset on long rest All slots (4× 1st, 2× 2nd) come back after a long rest (8 hours sleep). Nothing resets on a short rest except Wild Shape uses.

Guidance Conc
School Divination Time 1 action Range Touch Duration Conc, 1 min Components V, S
You touch one willing creature. Once before the spell ends, the target can roll a d4 and add the number rolled to one ability check of its choice. It can roll the die before or after making the ability check. The spell then ends.
Mending
School Transmutation Time 1 minute Range Touch Duration Instantaneous Components V, S, M (two lodestones)
This spell repairs a single break or tear in an object you touch, such as a broken chain link, two halves of a broken key, a torn cloak, or a leaking wineskin. As long as the break or tear is no larger than 1 foot in any dimension, you mend it, leaving no trace of the former damage. This spell can physically repair a magic item or construct, but the spell can’t restore magic to such an object.
1st Level — 4 slots — prepare up to 6 total across all levels
