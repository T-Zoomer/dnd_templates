Cantrips
Guidance Conc
School Divination Time 1 action Range Touch Duration Conc, 1 min Components V, S
You touch one willing creature. Once before the spell ends, the target can roll a d4 and add the number rolled to one ability check of its choice. It can roll the die before or after making the ability check. The spell then ends.
Mending
School Transmutation Time 1 minute Range Touch Duration Instantaneous Components V, S, M (two lodestones)
This spell repairs a single break or tear in an object you touch, such as a broken chain link, two halves of a broken key, a torn cloak, or a leaking wineskin. As long as the break or tear is no larger than 1 foot in any dimension, you mend it, leaving no trace of the former damage. This spell can physically repair a magic item or construct, but the spell can't restore magic to such an object.

School Conjuration Time 1 action Range 90 ft Duration Conc, 1 min Components V, S
Grasping weeds and vines sprout in a 20-foot square from a point within range. The area is difficult terrain for the duration. Creatures in the area when cast must make a Strength save or be restrained. A restrained creature can use its action to make a Strength check vs your spell save DC to free itself.

School Evocation Time 1 action Range 60 ft Duration Conc, 1 min Components V

Goodberry
School Transmutation Time 1 action Range Touch Duration Instantaneous Components V, S, M (sprig of mistletoe)
Up to ten berries appear in your hand, infused with magic. A creature can use its action to eat one berry, restoring 1 HP and providing nourishment for one day. Berries lose potency after 24 hours.

Animal Friendship Wis save
School Enchantment Time 1 action Range 30 ft Duration 24 hours Components V, S, M (morsel of food)
Choose a beast you can see within range. If its Intelligence is 4 or higher, the spell fails. Otherwise, the beast must succeed on a Wisdom save or be charmed by you for the duration. Ends if you or your companions harm the target.
Higher levels: +1 additional beast per slot level above 1st.
Beast Bond Conc
School Divination Time 1 action Range Touch Duration Conc, 10 min Components V, S, M (bit of fur in cloth)
You establish a telepathic link with a beast you touch that is friendly or charmed. Fails if the beast's Intelligence is 4 or higher. While in line of sight, the beast understands your telepathic messages and can communicate simple emotions back. The beast gains advantage on attacks against creatures within 5 ft of you that you can see.
Charm Person Wis save
School Enchantment Time 1 action Range 30 ft Duration 1 hour Components V, S
A humanoid you can see within range must make a Wisdom save (with advantage if you or allies are fighting it). On a fail, it is charmed until the spell ends or until you harm it. The charmed creature regards you as a friendly acquaintance. It knows it was charmed when the spell ends.
Higher levels: +1 target per slot level above 1st (must be within 30 ft of each other).
Create or Destroy Water
School Transmutation Time 1 action Range 30 ft Duration Instantaneous Components V, S, M (drop of water or grains of sand)
Create: Up to 10 gallons of clean water in an open container, or rain in a 30-ft cube extinguishing exposed flames. Destroy: Destroy up to 10 gallons of water in an open container, or destroy fog in a 30-ft cube.
Higher levels: +10 gallons or +5 ft cube per slot level above 1st.

School Divination Time 1 action Range Self Duration Conc, 10 min Components V, S, M (yew leaf)
For the duration, you can sense the presence and location of poisons, poisonous creatures, and diseases within 30 ft. You also identify the kind of poison, poisonous creature, or disease in each case.

Snare Dex save
School Abjuration Time 1 minute Range Touch Duration 8 hours (until triggered) Components S, M (25 ft of rope, consumed)
You create a nearly invisible magic trap in a 5-ft circle. Requires Intelligence (Investigation) vs your spell save DC to spot. Triggers when a Small/Medium/Large creature moves onto it: Dexterity save or hoisted upside down 3 ft in the air and restrained. Restrained creature can repeat the save at end of each turn, or someone in reach can make an Intelligence (Arcana) check vs your spell DC to free it.
