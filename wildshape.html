How Wild Shape Works
▶
Activate Use your bonus action to transform (Circle of the Moon feature). You pick which beast form to take.
▶
Your HP becomes the beast’s HP While transformed you use the beast’s hit points. If those drop to 0 you revert back to your normal form with whatever HP you had before transforming. You do not take the leftover damage.
▶
Your stats become the beast’s stats You use the beast’s STR, DEX, CON, AC, speed, and attacks. You keep your own WIS, INT, CHA and your proficiency bonus. You keep your own personality and memories.
▶
No spellcasting You can’t cast spells while in beast form. If you were concentrating on a spell before transforming, you can keep concentrating on it.
▶
Your gear merges or drops Equipment either merges into the beast form or drops to the ground — your DM decides. Magic items generally stop functioning while merged.
▶
Ending the form You can revert back at any time, no action needed. You also revert if you fall unconscious or the beast’s HP drops to 0.
▶
Limits Max beast CR: 1. No flying speed. No swimming speed (unlocks at druid level 4).
⚔ Combat Wild Shape — Circle of the Moon
While you are transformed, you can use a bonus action to expend one of your druid spell slots and recover hit points.

Roll 1d8 for every level of the spell slot you expend, then add that many HP to your current beast form HP.

Example: expend a 1st-level slot → roll 1d8, heal that many HP. Can’t go above the beast’s maximum HP.
